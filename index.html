How a mirror finish happens

Every part goes through the same steps, from coarse to fine. Skipping one is how you end up with swirl marks.

01 · Inspect

Strip & prep

Remove clear coat, paint and oxidation. Check for pitting and damage.

02 · 180–400 grit

Sand

Level scratches and casting marks until the surface is uniform.

03 · 600–1500 grit

Refine

Finer sanding takes out the marks left by the coarse pass.

04 · Tripoli

Cut buff

A sisal or spiral wheel with cutting compound brings up the first shine.

05 · Rouge

Color buff

A soft cotton wheel with fine rouge leaves the final mirror, then we seal it.
